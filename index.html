Real-time glass, in five terms.

Iridescence — Thin-film wave interference that shifts color with view angle. Essential for soap bubbles, butterfly wings, and anti-reflective optical coatings.

Index of refraction — The ratio of light speed in vacuum to the medium. Governs Fresnel reflectance at normal incidence and the angle of Snellian bending.

Dispersion — The variation of IOR with wavelength, producing chromatic separation at refracted boundaries. Quantified by the Abbe number.

Transmission — Specular passage through the medium without subsurface scattering. Attenuation follows the Beer-Lambert law across the ray path.

Roughness — Microfacet distribution that scatters specular reflection and refraction, transitioning polished crystal into frosted etched glass.
